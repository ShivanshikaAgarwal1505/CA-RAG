**SOLUTION PLAN:**
To cater to the family's requirements, I suggest a 6-day trip to Jim Corbett National Park and Nainital, Uttarakhand. This destination offers a mix of educational, adventurous, and kid-friendly activities.

Day 1-2: Jim Corbett National Park
- Stay at a kid-friendly resort like Corbett Jungle Resort or The Riverview Retreat, which offer activities like nature walks, bird watching, and outdoor games.
- Take a guided jungle safari to spot animals like tigers, elephants, and deer. This will be an exciting and educational experience for the kids.
- Visit the Corbett Falls and engage in a nature walk to learn about the local flora and fauna.

Day 3-4: Nainital
- Stay at a family-friendly hotel like The Manu Maharani or Hotel Himalaya, which offer comfortable rooms and amenities like game rooms and kids' play areas.
- Take a ride on the Nainital Ropeway, which offers breathtaking views of the surrounding hills and lakes.
- Visit the Nainital Zoo, which is home to a variety of animals like tigers, bears, and monkeys. The kids will love the interactive sessions and animal feeding activities.
- Engage in boating and paddle-boating at the Naini Lake, which will be a fun and relaxing experience for the family.

Day 5-6: Bhimtal and surrounding areas
- Visit the Bhimtal Lake and take a boat ride to the nearby island, which has a beautiful aquarium and a variety of fish species.
- Take a short trek to the nearby villages and engage in activities like pottery-making and traditional crafts.
- Visit the High Altitude Zoo at Bhimtal, which is home to a variety of animals like red pandas, snow leopards, and Himalayan black bears.

**TRADE-OFFS:**
To stay within the budget, I've compromised on the following:
- Instead of staying at luxury resorts, I've opted for mid-range, kid-friendly stays that offer comfortable amenities and activities.
- I've skipped visiting museums and historical sites, as per the kids' preference, and focused on outdoor activities and animal-related experiences.
