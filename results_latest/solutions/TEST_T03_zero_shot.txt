**SOLUTION PLAN:**
For a family of 2 adults and 2 kids (6 and 9 years old) with a budget of ₹35,000 for 6 days, I recommend a trip to Jim Corbett National Park in Uttarakhand, India. This destination offers a mix of educational and adventure activities that cater to the kids' love for animals.

Day 1-2: Arrival and Acclimatization
- Stay at a kid-friendly resort like the Corbett Jungle Club or similar, which offers basic amenities and is safe for children.
- Explore the surroundings, visit the Corbett Falls, and engage in nature walks to get accustomed to the environment.

Day 3: Wildlife Safari
- Take a guided safari tour in the Corbett National Park to spot various wildlife, including tigers, elephants, and deer. This will be an educational experience for the kids, teaching them about different species and their habitats.

Day 4: Adventure Activities
- Organize a river rafting or kayaking trip (depending on the water level and safety) in the Kosi River. This will provide an adrenaline rush for both kids and adults.
- Visit the Garjiya Devi Temple, which is situated on a hill and offers a scenic view of the river.

Day 5: Elephant Safari and Conservation
- Book an elephant safari, which allows the family to get up close with these magnificent creatures. This activity will not only be enjoyable but also educational, as the kids will learn about elephant conservation.
- Visit a local village to understand the daily life and traditions of the people living near the national park.

Day 6: Departure
- Spend the morning relaxing at the resort or taking a short trek.
- Depart for home in the afternoon.

**TRADE-OFFS:**
- The budget might be a bit tight for all the activities and accommodations, so some compromises on luxury or additional expenses might be necessary.
- Since the kids dislike museums, we've avoided any such visits, focusing instead on outdoor and interactive learning experiences.
