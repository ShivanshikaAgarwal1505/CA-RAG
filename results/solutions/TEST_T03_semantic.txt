**Why Coorg?**  
- **Elephant & wildlife focus** (Dubare camp, Nagarhole).  
- **Cool climate**, short drive from Bangalore → minimal travel fatigue.  
- **Plenty of kid‑friendly homestays** that serve home‑cooked meals and have a garden for safe play.
