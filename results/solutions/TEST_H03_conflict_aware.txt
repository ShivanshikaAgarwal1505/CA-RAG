### 2️⃣ Prep‑once‑a‑week (≈ 30 min on weekend)

| Item | How to prepare | Storage | Portion per day |
|------|----------------|---------|-----------------|
| **Boiled chickpeas & mixed beans** | Soak ½ kg dry chickpeas & ½ kg mixed beans overnight; pressure‑cook 2 whistles; keep in fridge (up to 4 days) | Airtight container | ½ cup each meal |
| **Sprouted moong** | Rinse 100 g moong, soak 6 h, drain, keep in a jar at room temp, rinse 2×/day; ready in 1 day | Jar with cloth cover | ½ cup per dinner |
| **Roasted peanuts & pumpkin‑seed butter** | Roast 200 g peanuts with a pinch salt; grind 100 g pumpkin seeds with a dash water to make spread | Small jars | 30 g peanuts, 1 tbsp butter |
| **Ground flax & chia mix** | Mix equal parts; store dry | Small zip‑bag | 1 tbsp per meal |
| **Cinnamon & fenugreek water** | Keep ground cinnamon; each morning stir ½ tsp in warm water (optional) | – | – |

All steps require **no cooking at the hostel** – you only heat the pre‑cooked legumes in a microwave or on the mess stovetop for < 2 min.
