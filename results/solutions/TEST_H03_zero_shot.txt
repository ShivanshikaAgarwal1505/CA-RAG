### Daily Routine Tips (≤10 min per meal)

1. **Batch‑cook** brown rice, mixed dal, and moong dal every 2‑3 days in a small electric pressure‑cooker or rice cooker. Store in airtight containers in the hostel fridge.
2. **Pre‑soak** moong dal for 30 min the night before; it cooks in ≤5 min under pressure.
3. Keep **ready‑to‑eat snacks** (roasted chana, nuts, pumpkin seeds) in small zip‑locks for quick grab‑and‑go.
4. Use a **microwave** or electric kettle to heat oats, rice, and khichdi – no stovetop needed.
5. Add a squeeze of **lemon** or a sprinkle of **cinnamon** for flavor without sugar.
