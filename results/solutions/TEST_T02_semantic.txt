### Why Nainital?

1. **Mountain vibe** – Nestled in the Kumaon Himalayas, cool December evenings (‑2 °C to 8 °C) with occasional snowfall give a genuine honeymoon feel.  
2. **Luxury feel without 5‑star price tag** – *The Naini Retreat* is consistently rated 4.5 / 5 on major travel sites, offers a private garden, jacuzzi, lake‑view terrace, and a full‑service spa. It is the **closest “near‑5‑star” experience** in a hill‑station that can be negotiated to **≈ 5 500 / night** (incl. meals).  
3. **Privacy** – Only 30 rooms, many with private entrances; the resort can block out adjacent rooms on request.  
4. **Accessibility** – Direct flights to Dehradun keep travel time short; the 7‑hour scenic drive is comfortable in a private SUV.  
5. **Romantic & wellness facilities** – In‑house spa, private dining spaces, and optional yoga sessions.
