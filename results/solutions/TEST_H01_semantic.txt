#### How to make the plan work in a hostel mess

1. **Talk to the mess cook** (once a week) and ask:
   * Add a **small portion of soy‑chunks (soya nuggets)** to the dal or make a “soy‑dal” on a day of your choice.  
   * Include **leafy greens** (spinach, methi, sarson) in the vegetable mix at least **3 times a week**.  
   * Use **tomato or bell‑pepper** in the salad or veg curry for Vitamin C.

2. **Carry a small insulated bag** with your **snacks** (peanuts, dates, soy‑chunks, fruit). They need only soaking in water (for soy) or can be eaten straight.
