- **Animals first:** Safari, elephant centre, zoo, and rescued‑animal interactions give plenty of live‑animal exposure.
- **Adventure, not museums:** Zip‑line, rope‑course, boat ride, and nature walks keep them moving.
- **Learning opportunities:** Forest‑ranger talk, village‑school visit, guided nature walk teach ecology and conservation.

### Practical Tips

1. **Book train tickets early** (e‑ticket, sleeper class for comfort).  
2. **Reserve the jeep safari** at the park office a week in advance (online if possible).  
3. **Ask the guesthouse** to arrange the village‑school visit and nature‑walk – many homestays have tie‑ups with NGOs.  
4. **Carry cash** (small denominations) for entry fees and local transport; ATMs are limited in the park area.  
5. **Pack light**: quick‑dry clothing, rain jacket (monsoon season), sturdy shoes, hats, sunscreen, and a reusable water bottle.  
6. **Health safety:** Carry basic first‑aid kit, any child‑specific meds, and a copy of immunisation records.
