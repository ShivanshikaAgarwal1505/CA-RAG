### Practical Tips for Success
- **Book the train and resort together on a “bundle” site** – many sites give an extra 5 % off when both are booked simultaneously.  
- **Carry a light winter wardrobe** (thermal wear, gloves) to avoid renting gear at the resort (extra ₹1,500).  
- **Carry a small portable charger** – mountain resorts have spotty power; this avoids paying for a power‑bank rental.  
- **Confirm spa availability** ahead of time; December can be busy with locals, so pre‑booking is essential.  
- **Use cashless payments** (UPI/credit‑card) to earn reward points that can be redeemed for future trips.
