**SOLUTION PLAN:**
For a luxurious honeymoon getaway within a budget of ₹25,000 for 4 days in December, I recommend the following plan:

1. **Destination:** Manali, Himachal Pradesh. Manali offers a serene mountainous landscape, perfect for a romantic getaway. It's a bit more budget-friendly than other popular hill stations like Shimla or Mussoorie, making it an ideal choice given the constraints.

2. **Accommodation:** Book a 5-star luxury resort like the Solang Valley Resort or similar, which offers rooms with a valley view, a private balcony, and all modern amenities. Given the budget, opting for a slightly off-peak season package or a room that's not the most premium but still luxurious could be a good compromise. Cost approximately ₹18,000 - ₹20,000 for 3 nights, depending on the season and availability.

3. **Romantic Dinners:** The resort itself will likely have a fine dining restaurant where you can enjoy romantic dinners. Additionally, Manali has several restaurants that offer candlelit dinners with a beautiful view of the mountains. One such place is the Johnson Hotel's restaurant, which is known for its ambiance and food.

4. **Spa:** The 5-star resort will have a spa where you can indulge in couples' massages and other treatments. This will not only be relaxing but also add to the romantic ambiance of your honeymoon.

5. **Privacy:** To ensure privacy, choose a room that is located in a quieter part of the resort, possibly with a private sit-out area or balcony overlooking the valley. Early morning walks or evening strolls around the less crowded areas of Manali can also provide ample privacy and quality time together.

6. **Activities:** Besides relaxing at the resort, you can plan a romantic walk through the Old Manali village, visit the Hidimba Devi Temple, or take a short trek to a nearby viewpoint. These activities are low-cost or free, fitting well within the budget.

**TRADE-OFFS:**
- The primary compromise is that the most luxurious suites or villas at the top 5-star resorts might be out of budget. Instead, a slightly less premium room within a 5-star resort will be chosen.
- Another potential trade-off could be traveling during the early part of December, which is considered off-season in Manali, to get better deals on accommodations and packages.

**FEASIBILITY SCORE:** 8/10
This plan is feasible because it balances the desire for luxury and romance with the budget constraint. Manali offers a great blend of natural beauty, romantic settings, and luxury at a relatively affordable price compared to other hill stations. The only potential drawbacks are the weather (it can be quite cold in December) and the possibility of some tourist attractions being less accessible due to winter conditions. However, for a couple looking for a serene, cold-weather honeymoon experience, this should not be a significant deterrent.
